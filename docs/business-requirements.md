# Business Requirements

## 1. Background
Describe the business context of the report.

## 2. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder Group | Need | Usage Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Management | Track business performance | Daily/Weekly |
| Operations | Monitor operational issues | Daily |
| Analysts | Drill down and investigate metrics | Ad-hoc |

## 3. Business Questions

- What are the main KPIs stakeholders need to monitor?
- Which dimensions are required for drill-down?
- What filters are necessary?
- What business rules affect metric calculation?
- How often does the report need to refresh?

## 4. Success Criteria

- Report answers the agreed business questions.
- Metrics are clearly defined and validated.
- Report refresh is stable.
- Dataset size and refresh time are acceptable.
- Documentation is available for future maintenance.
